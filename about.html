About

I started my career in IT by doing what was needed to keep systems running, fixing issues as they arose, and learning to solve problems under pressure. Today, I work as an IT Assistant at English Press Limited as part of a two-person IT team supporting a wide range of infrastructure and end users. My responsibilities include assisting in managing Active Directory, Microsoft 365, network switches and routers, disaster recovery, IP telephony, and providing day-to-day technical support for the office staff.

To continue building my networking expertise, I am currently pursuing the Cisco CCNA through Cisco Networking Academy (NetAcad). My goal is to understand not only how to deploy and manage networks, but also how they are designed, secured, and interconnected. Building this strong networking foundation is an important step toward my long-term goal of a career in cybersecurity.

This portfolio reflects where I am in my professional journey. It showcases the projects I've worked on, the skills I've developed, and my continued growth in networking as I work toward a career in cybersecurity.
