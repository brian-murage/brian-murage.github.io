01About

I got into IT the practical way — fixing what's broken, keeping networks up, and figuring things out under pressure. I currently work as an ICT Support Technician at English Press Limited, where I handle everything from Active Directory and Microsoft 365 to network switches and IP telephony, in a two-person team that has to cover a lot of ground.

Lately I've been pulled toward the offensive side of security — I want to understand systems well enough to know how they break, not just how to keep them running. I'm currently on the Pentesting Track with Cyber Shujaa and working through CCNA labs on NetLabs, building the networking depth that good pentesting actually depends on.

I'm not claiming expertise I don't have yet — this site tracks where I'm at, what I've done, and what I'm still building.
